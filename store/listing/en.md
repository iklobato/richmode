## Full description (both stores, max 4000 characters)

RichMode is an entertainment app. It shows the home screen of a made-up account, with any balance you choose.

It is a simulation. RichMode is not a financial institution, moves no money, connects to no account and makes no payments or transfers. Every balance in the app carries a "Fictional balance" label that cannot be removed.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Pick the display name, the balance and the invested amount
• Show amounts in Brazilian real, US dollar or euro
• Switch between themes with made-up brands, instantly
• Hide and show the balance with one tap

PRIVACY
The app collects no data. No sign-up, no ads, no analytics. The amounts you choose stay on your device.

RESPONSIBLE USE
RichMode makes no receipts, statements, QR codes or payment keys. Using the app to fool anyone in business, credit or sales is forbidden by the terms of use: https://richmode.app/terms
