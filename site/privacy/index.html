Privacy policy

Last updated October 11, 2026.

Summary

The RichMode app does not collect any of your data. We have no server, we ask for no sign-up, and we use no analytics, ads or tracking tools.

What stays on your device

The display name, amounts, currency and theme you pick are stored only on your device, so the app remembers them next time. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. To erase it, uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to the camera, photos, contacts, location, microphone or notifications.

This website

richmode.app uses no cookies, forms or analytics, and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log the IP address of visitors for security, as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Privacy questions: suporte@richmode.app.
