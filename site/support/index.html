FAQ

Is RichMode a real account?

No. It is an entertainment app. No amount is real, no money moves and the app connects to no financial institution.

Can I remove the "Fictional balance" label?

No. The label always sits next to the balance, in every theme, so nobody mistakes the screen for a real account.

Where are the amounts I choose stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.

How do I go back to the starting values?

Open "Edit values" (pencil icon) and change what you want. To erase everything, uninstall and reinstall the app.

Why do Pix, Transfer and Pay do nothing?

Because it is a simulation. They only show the notice "This is a simulation. Nothing real happens here."

Someone used the app to fool me. What should I do?

Do not trust any "balance" shown as proof in business, credit or sales. Check amounts directly with your financial institution. If needed, report it to the police and write to us with details.
