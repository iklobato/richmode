RICHMODE Português

Any balance you want. On screen only.

RichMode is an entertainment app. It shows the home screen of a made-up account, with any balance you choose.

Notice: RichMode is a simulation.

It is not a financial institution, it moves no money and it connects to no account. Every balance in the app carries a "Fictional balance" label that cannot be removed.

What you can do

  • Pick the display name, the balance and the invested amount, in BRL, USD or EUR.
  • Switch between themes with made-up brands, instantly.
  • Hide and show the balance with one tap.
  • Use it with no sign-up: nothing leaves your device.

What you cannot do

RichMode does not make receipts, statements, QR codes or payment keys, and no button makes a payment or a transfer. Using the app to fool anyone in business, credit or sales is forbidden by the terms of use.
